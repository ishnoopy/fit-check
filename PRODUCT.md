# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TUFF is for people tracking workouts on a phone while they are training. They need to log sets, see today's progress, resume active sessions, and understand consistency without reading dense reports.

## Product Purpose

The product helps users build training consistency through quick workout logging, program structure, streak feedback, and simple progress views. Success means a user can glance, act, and return to training without cognitive drag.

## Brand Personality

Chunky, warm, direct. The interface should feel like a friendly training console: bold enough to motivate, simple enough to use between sets, and playful without becoming childish.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic white-and-teal fitness dashboards, cold SaaS admin panels, thin outline-only mobile UI, glassmorphism, purple gradients, and decorative card grids. Do not let the mascot or playful palette reduce task clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action obvious.
2. Use large, sturdy shapes that survive quick glances on mobile.
3. Let progress read visually before it reads numerically.
4. Keep surfaces warm and tactile, not sterile.
5. Use playful character and color as reinforcement, not decoration that competes with workout tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Avoid relying on color alone for workout status. Keep tap targets at least 44px where practical. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep animations short, functional, and non-blocking.
